Our story

Made to be worn every day, not saved for later.

The name Mirazhé comes from two ideas we love: mira, to look, and zhé, a soft sound that sounds like shimmer. Together they mean something you look at twice.

We keep our range small on purpose. Each piece of jewellery is tested for tarnish and skin reaction, and each cosmetic is tested on a range of skin tones before it ships.

Skin-safe metals

Nickel-free, hypoallergenic findings on every piece.

Shades for deshi skin

Small batches

Limited runs, so what you get is fresh and carefully finished.
